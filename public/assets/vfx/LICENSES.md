# VFX asset licenses

## Unity Labs pyro flipbooks

Files derived from the free Unity Labs VFX image sequences:

- `pyro/explosion-02hd-5x5.png` from `Explosion02HD_5x5.tga`
- `pyro/explosion-light-5x5.png` from `Explosion01-light_5x5.tga`
- `pyro/fireball-03-8x8.png` from `FireBall03_8x8.tga`
- `pyro/disc-smoke-16x4.png` from `DiscSmoke01_16x4.tga`

Source: https://unity.com/blog/engine-platform/free-vfx-image-sequences-flipbooks

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal). Unity Technologies released the image sequences and flipbooks under CC0 for unrestricted project use.

Runtime derivatives were converted to PNG and capped at 1024 pixels on their longest edge. The original EXR/TGA source archives are intentionally not shipped with the game.
